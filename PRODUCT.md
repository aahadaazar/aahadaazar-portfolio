# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Recruiters and hiring teams evaluating Aahad for software and product engineering roles.
- Founders and product teams looking for an engineering collaborator or consultant.
- Engineers and other potential collaborators exploring Aahad's work, writing, and areas of expertise.

Visitors need to understand Aahad's capabilities, see credible examples of his work, and reach the relevant profile or contact channel.

## Product Purpose

This is Aahad Aazar's developer portfolio. It presents his experience, technical expertise, projects, and writing so prospective employers, clients, and collaborators can evaluate his work and connect with him.

Success means a visitor can quickly understand what kind of engineer Aahad is, find evidence relevant to their needs, and continue the conversation through LinkedIn, GitHub, Medium, or email.

## Positioning

Aahad is a Product Engineer with combined expertise in Data Engineering, AI Engineering, and Software Engineering. The portfolio should demonstrate the ability to connect these disciplines in working products rather than presenting them as isolated specialties.

## Operating Context

The portfolio is publicly browsed as a professional introduction and evaluation surface. Visitors may scan work history and skills, inspect featured project repositories, read published writing, use the embedded AI assistant, or follow a direct contact link.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Present Aahad's professional experience, with his current role stated as **Software Engineer at Scalers**.
- Present his frontend, backend, AI/LLM, data, infrastructure, and testing experience without implying mastery or outcomes not supported by evidence.
- Link to featured project repositories and describe their demonstrated product purpose and technologies.
- Surface recent Medium writing from the existing feed integration.
- Provide direct links to LinkedIn, GitHub, Medium, and email.
- Offer the existing embedded Aahad AI Assistant as an additional way to explore the portfolio.
- Preserve the site as a responsive web experience with light and dark themes.
- Do not invent employers, clients, testimonials, performance results, project adoption, business outcomes, or credentials.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the name **Aahad Aazar**.
- Use **Product Engineer** as the primary professional positioning.
- Treat Data Engineering, AI Engineering, and Software Engineering as the three defining areas of expertise.
- Keep the voice direct, technically credible, and grounded in real work.
- Preserve the existing Aahad favicon/mark unless a future request explicitly replaces it (`public/favicon.svg` and related icon assets).

## Product Principles

1. Lead with product-engineering breadth while making each specialty concrete through real work.
2. Make credibility inspectable: connect claims to projects, employment history, writing, or public profiles.
3. Help different audiences find their path without turning the portfolio into separate experiences.
4. Keep contact and deeper exploration close at hand throughout the experience.
5. Prefer accurate, specific evidence over inflated claims or exhaustive technology lists.
